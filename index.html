Carrefour signs a toy import contract with a Chinese manufacturer.

Carrefour, the French retail group, wants to import a shipment of toys from Labubu, a manufacturer based in Shenzhen, China. Each side wants to invoice in its own currency to avoid the exchange rate risk. The contract is payable at 90 days, as is common practice in B2B trade.

This time, the French buyer (Carrefour) wins the negotiation: the contract will be invoiced in €, at a rate of 1€ = ¥7.80 on the day of signature.
